Keyboard restoration

Recovery of antique, time-worn keyboards: new coverings, repair of key levers and balance rails, cleaning or replacement of pins, and repairs in general.

What we do

Depending on the condition of the keyboard and the client's needs, we restore existing keyboards by replacing components, key bushings in woollen cloth, coverings, pins or other parts.

We approach restoration with respect for the original construction of each component, working in a conservative way wherever possible.

Antique ivory keyboards can be bleached and have their lustre restored, or partial or complete replacement of components can be considered.
