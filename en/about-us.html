A tradition that continues

Our story begins in 2006, when Andrea Colina joined a workshop to learn the trade of piano tuner and technician. Over the years he worked both as an employee and on his own account, collaborating with many local companies in concert service, restoration and tuning.

In 2023 he took over the legacy of Carlo Gandolfo, owner of Gandolfo Tastiere, the workshop he had relied on for years and from which he received the craft of keyboard making. From that moment A.R.C. has devoted itself almost exclusively to making and restoring keyboards for pianos, organs, harpsichords, spinets and other instruments.

We are the third generation of a line of makers that began with the Barra brothers and continued with Gandolfo. We work with fine woods, bone and traditional materials to give every musician a precise touch and a mechanism that is always ready and responsive.
