About Us

A.R.C. is the third generation of a line of keyboard makers: the expertise of the Barra brothers passed to Carlo Gandolfo ("Gandolfo Tastiere") and continues here today, so that a historic craft is not lost.

Since 2023 we have been making and restoring keyboards for pianos, organs, harpsichords and spinets, combining research and innovation with the knowledge inherited from the workshops that came before us.
