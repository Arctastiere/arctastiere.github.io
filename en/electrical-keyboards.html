Keyboards with electrical fittings

Keyboards supplied complete with magnets, springs for key movement and electrical fittings such as sensors or contacts, ready for connection to the electronic control systems of organs.

What we make

On existing keyboards or on our own models we install DAMINATO spring systems for key repetition. We also fit each key with a system of adjustable magnets, which makes it possible to adjust the weight of the key.

Installation also includes fitting contact systems, again DAMINATO, or sensors by ELTEC or TERMISOUND, so that the keyboard can be connected to electronic control systems.

As an alternative to springs, we also make our more compact keyboards, the MARTA model. For key repetition they use only a system of magnets, which allows the key to be removed from the frame without having to detach it from the spring.

All of these keyboards can be made with the woods and coverings requested by the client (Plexiglas, PVC, Corian, bone, ebony, boxwood or other woods).
