New keyboards

We make custom keyboards, as copies or to the client's design.

What we make

We build our keyboards with the best materials available, respecting historical specifications where requested.

  • Keyboards pivoted at the rear, or balanced on a balance rail
  • Keys in Norway spruce, walnut or other woods
  • Modern frames with stainless steel pins, or antique-style frames with brass pins
  • Key guides below, behind or between the keys

The frames are made of hardwoods such as beech or walnut, or of spruce with hardwood inserts for the pins.

We also make plain or shaped cheek pieces, fitted where required with hinges for stacking keyboards in organ consoles.

Materials

The key coverings can be made of plastic materials or bone for the white keys, or of fine wood.

  • White plastic material (Plexiglas or PVC)
  • Fine mineral-based material (Corian or Elforin)
  • Top-quality bone
  • Wood: boxwood (Brazilian or European), ebony or other woods

The sharps too can be made in different woods, such as ebony, bone-covered ebony, walnut, pearwood or other woods on request.
