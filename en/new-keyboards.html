New keyboards

We make custom keyboards, copied from originals or built to the client's design.

What we make

We build our keyboards with the best materials available, following historical specifications where required.

  • Keyboards with tail-pivoted keys or keys on a balance rail
  • Keys in Norway spruce, walnut or other woods
  • Modern frames with stainless steel pins, or antique-style frames with brass pins
  • Key guides beneath, behind or between the keys

Frames are made of hardwoods such as beech or walnut, or of spruce with hardwood inserts for the pins.

We also make plain or shaped cheek pieces, with hinges where needed for stacking the keyboards in the console.

Materials

Natural keys can be covered in plastic materials or bone, or in fine woods.

  • White plastic (Plexiglas or PVC)
  • High-quality mineral composite (Corian or Elforin)
  • Top-grade bone
  • Wood: boxwood (Brazilian or European), ebony or other woods

Sharps can also be made in various woods, such as ebony, bone-capped ebony, walnut, pear or other woods on request.
